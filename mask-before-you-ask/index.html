● Privacy by design

The safest server is the one that never receives your text.

The site is delivered by Cloudflare, but the words you paste are checked by code already running in your browser. They are not uploaded, stored, or used to train a model.

  • 01
    No account

    There is no sign-in and no personal profile.

  • 02
    No text history

    The app does not keep previous checks.

  • 03
    No AI analysis

    Transparent browser rules find common patterns.

  • 04
    No false promise

    A final human review is still necessary.

Know the limit

A useful filter—not a guarantee.

The checker can notice common formats such as email addresses, phone numbers, payment-card-like numbers, internet addresses, private links, dates, and labeled account references. Context is messy, so review every mark and add any person, place, or project name you want hidden.
