# Frozen evaluation rubric

This rubric was frozen after the first working build and before Variant B changes. Both variants use the same criteria and weights.

The evaluation is an expert heuristic exercise, not a human-subject study. A rating from 0–5 is multiplied by the dimension weight. Scores require screenshot, test, copy, or measured evidence; impressions without evidence do not count.

| Dimension | Weight | A 5/5 means |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Comprehension in five seconds | 15 | A first-time, non-technical visitor can identify the audience, job, and restraint-first premise from the opening viewport. |
| Trust | 15 | Boundaries, score mechanics, data behavior, affiliations, and limitations are plain, specific, and not overstated. |
| Visual memorability | 15 | The identity is distinct, coherent, legible, responsive, and meaningfully connected to the product idea. |
| Usefulness | 15 | The questions and output support a real decision without writing content, promising outcomes, or hiding judgment. |
| Task completion | 15 | The primary journey is obvious, proportionate in effort, resilient to errors/network loss, and produces actionable closure. |
| Accessibility | 10 | Semantics, keyboard flow, contrast, reduced motion, no-script reading, zoom/mobile layout, and status communication hold up. |
| Performance | 10 | The production build is lean, locally responsive, free of avoidable requests, and has no obvious rendering bottleneck. |
| GitHub exploration | 5 | A visitor can understand, verify, run, and review the project within 30 seconds of opening its folder. |

## Rating anchors

- **5 — exceptional:** no material issue; evidence is strong and repeatable.
- **4 — good:** works well; one or two bounded issues remain.
- **3 — adequate:** usable, but a meaningful issue reduces confidence or completion.
- **2 — weak:** several material problems or one severe problem.
- **1 — poor:** the dimension is barely addressed.
- **0 — absent or broken:** the dimension cannot be evaluated successfully.

## Severity model

- **High:** blocks responsible use, accessibility, privacy truthfulness, core completion, or reviewability.
- **Medium:** materially increases confusion, time, error risk, or friction but has a workaround.
- **Low:** bounded polish or maintainability issue with little effect on a responsible decision.
