          What forces “Stay quiet”?

          Rules say no, the topic is high-stakes, the intent is promotion, or there is not enough information.

          What can limit the score?

          Unknown rules, no unmet need, possible sensitivity, or an answer that requires a click.

          What subtracts points?

          A crowded conversation, mixed motives, click-dependent help, or missing context.
