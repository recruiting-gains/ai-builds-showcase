          Rules before reach

          If you enter, leave the room better.

          This guide does not tell you what to write. It sets the conditions for respectful participation.

          1. 01

            Read every community rule

            Check whether business participation is allowed, restricted, or prohibited. The rules outrank this score.

          2. 02

            Observe the tone

            Notice how established members answer, what moderators remove, and whether the room wants expert input.

          3. 03

            Answer the actual question

            Do not redirect the discussion toward your services, your story, or a problem nobody asked you to solve.

          4. 04

            Make the answer complete in-room

            The useful part should stand on its own without a link, click, purchase, follow-up, or private conversation.

          5. 05

            Disclose your affiliation

            State your business connection plainly when it matters. Let readers weigh your perspective with full context.

          6. 06

            Avoid unsolicited sales messages

            Do not turn a public question into a private sales approach. Silence is safer than unwanted contact.

          7. 07

            Respect moderators

            If a moderator redirects or removes participation, accept the decision without argument or workarounds.

          8. 08

            Contribute consistently

            Trust comes from being useful over time, not appearing only when attention might benefit your business.

          No black box

          The score is arithmetic you can audit.

          Seven positive factors add to 100. Guardrails can subtract points, cap the result, or stop the decision entirely.

          • Helpfulness gap24
          • Community-rule fit22
          • Business relevance16
          • Trust opportunity12
          • Conversation momentum10

          • Geographic fit8
          What forces “Stay quiet”?

          Prohibited business participation, a sensitive or high-stakes topic, promotion as the main intent, or insufficient information creates an exclusion and a final score of zero.

          What can cap the score?

          Unknown rules, no helpfulness gap, possible sensitivity, or value that requires a click caps the score at 34. An already-met need or incomplete context caps it at 54.

          What creates a penalty?

          A saturated conversation subtracts 18 points; mixed intent subtracts 20; partly self-contained help subtracts 8; click-dependent value subtracts 25; and context gaps subtract 12.

          A deliberately small data footprint

          The discussion stays where it is.

          NO MEGAPHONE does not connect to accounts, identify people, visit pages, collect usernames, read discussions, or write checklist answers to an application database.

          Your broad work context can be kept in this browser so you do not repeat setup. It is never included in the scoring request, and you can delete it here.

          If this project is deployed later, the hosting provider may process standard security and operational metadata under its own policies. The application itself creates no answer history.
