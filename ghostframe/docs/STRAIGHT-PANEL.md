# Straight HandFrame panel

Color worlds default to **Panel edges → Straight panel** with **Follow my hands** enabled. Keep both thumbs and index fingers apart. Those four tips move, stretch and tilt one flat projective surface; curling intermediate finger joints does not create inward notches. Bring both palms together and reopen to change color as before.

The supplied recording showed the original contour bending inward around thumb/index joints. That implementation intentionally included up to fourteen joint vertices. The new panel uses exactly four measured corners and rejects collapsed/crossed/nonconvex arrangements instead of drawing a folded or notched shape. It does not infer extra depth from palm size: the measured corners already determine the visible perspective. The original **Follow finger contour** option remains available, including its optional Center depth behavior. Saved shapes, custom outlines, local pictures, Invisible and Cube retain their separate paths.

## Tracking and boundaries

Corners are filtered once per inference with elapsed time. Small tremor is damped; larger deliberate motion follows promptly. Detector order and either inverted L are supported. A valid detector omission freezes the last measurement for at most 150 ms; the compositor also expires panel state if inference stops delivering fresh results. Invalid corners, aspect changes, tracking loss, mode/manual/camera/source/edge-policy boundaries and reset require a fresh valid pair. Pre-boundary camera results cannot re-arm the panel. A stale hidden panel cannot supply a newly prepared still.

Four straight edges describe a convex quadrilateral, which may be a trapezoid when tilted. This is not a physical 3D reconstruction. Closing a thumb against its index finger can collapse one side and hide the panel; reopen the four corners to restore it. Real tracking remains affected by occlusion and lighting.

The texture and outline use the existing final Canvas2D compositor, so Record includes the panel in the saved clip. The original camera, MediaPipe worker, recorder and explicit still-upload endpoint remain shared. No dependency, provider request or infrastructure binding is added. The panel feature itself sends no camera media to a service.
